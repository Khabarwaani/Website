Product

Two engines. One reel every few minutes.

1. AI Scripting Agent

Reads primary sources (wires, PIB, court orders), drafts a hook-first Hindi script, and links every claim to its source. An editor approves or rewrites it.

2. Hindi Video Builder

Turns the approved script into a 9:16 reel with Devanagari captions and natural Hindi voice. No Premiere, no crew.

Where we are today

Both engines are in active development; the pilot is where we test them in public. Next: faster sign-off, better voice, a second language.

Business Model

How Khabarwaani makes money

1

Audience first (now)

Daily reels on Instagram and YouTube Shorts. Zero-CAC distribution; we earn the habit before we earn a rupee.

2

Native brand sponsorships

Brands pay to sit next to news people actually forward. The Morning Brew playbook, rebuilt for 9:16. Not dependent on India's low ad CPMs.

3

App & engine licensing

Move loyal viewers to an owned app, and license the scripting + video engine to regional publishers who can't afford studios.

Risks We Lose Sleep Over

The hard questions, answered up front

"What if the AI gets a fact wrong?"
No reel ships without human sign-off. Every line is linked to its source, and corrections are published, not buried.
"You're renting Instagram's audience."
True for now. We publish on Instagram and YouTube Shorts, and the owned app is phase 3 of the model.
"Satire + politics + India = legal risk."
We build to the IT Rules 2021 code for digital news publishers, punch at situations rather than people, and will run a public grievance channel.
"Indian ad CPMs are tiny."
That's why sponsorships and engine licensing come before platform ad revenue.
