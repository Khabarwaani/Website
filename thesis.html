Why Khabarwaani Was Started & What We Are Building

Khabarwaani was started from an unavoidable observation: news has become an exhausting, panic-inducing shouting match that viewers actively avoid. Why does news need to be verbose? Why can’t news be interesting like reels—and funny?

We have not completed this journey—we are in the active process of building it. Our goal is to replace screaming panels with 15s–60s fact-checked vertical reels across Indian languages, delivering curiosity, wit, and truth natively to mobile feeds.

The Founding Thesis

Why It Was Started & What We Are Building to Solve

Four structural realities ignited this project and define the destination we are working towards.

Catalyst 01 · The Crisis 39% NEWS AVOIDANCE

Why It Started: The Avoidance Paradox

We started because 39% of global citizens actively avoid news out of mental exhaustion from manufactured outrage. Our building goal: Prove that daily current affairs can inform without depressing or patronizing the audience.

Deep Dive: Media Crisis & Paradox →
Catalyst 02 · The Migration 350M+ FEED USERS

Why It Started: The Feed Migration

We started because linear TV has aged out (55+ median viewer age) while youth spend their hours on vertical smartphone feeds. Our building goal: Meet 350M+ Indians where their attention already lives with mobile-native 9:16 reels.

Deep Dive: Gen Z & Feed Migration →
Catalyst 03 · The Psychology POSITIVE EMOTION MOAT

Why It Started: Hostility vs. Wit

We started because newsrooms mistakenly equated virality with screaming and humiliation. Our building goal: Pioneer positive emotion, smart comedy, and relatable analogies as a superior, high-retention distribution moat.

Deep Dive: Reels & Positive Emotion →
Catalyst 04 · The Economics 90% LOWER COSTS

Why It Started: The Bureau Cost Trap

We started because traditional bureaus spend millions on physical studios and delayed production cycles. Our building goal: Construct an autonomous multi-agent mesh that produces verified, multilingual news reels in minutes at 10x lower costs.

The Structural Reset

The Broken Old Model vs. The Building Destination

Why cable broadcasting forced our founding—and the specific goal we are engineering towards.

What Forced Us to Start

  • ✕ Manufactured Outrage: Pitting screaming panellists against each other for TRPs
  • ✕ Exhausting Verbosity: 40 minutes of talking heads to communicate 30s of facts
  • ✕ Vanity Overhead: Enormous budgets squandered on physical broadcast studios
  • ✕ Public Cynicism: Driving 39% of the population into active news avoidance

The Destination We Are Building

  • ✓ Positive Emotion: Relatable wit and humor that make citizens enjoy learning facts
  • ✓ Atomic Brevity: 15s–60s crisp vertical explainers with zero throat-clearing fluff
  • ✓ Agentic Velocity: 90% cost cut, <5 min turnaround, and gazette fact-checking
  • ✓ Democratic Trust: Rebuilding everyday curiosity across Indian regional languages
