The Core Problem

Why Khabarwaani

People have unprecedented access to news, yet millions actively avoid it. The problem is no longer access to information—it is the exhausting, adversarial way news is delivered.

Understand the Problem Our Approach
1. The Human Problem

The News Paradox: More Access, Less Engagement

Information is now ubiquitous. News alerts flash on phones every hour, cable channels broadcast around the clock, and digital feeds never stop scrolling.

Yet access has not translated into understanding or trust. Instead, a growing share of the population is actively tuning out.

Documented Finding: 39% Selective News Avoidance

According to the Reuters Institute Digital News Report, an all-time high of 39% of respondents say they often or sometimes actively avoid news, citing emotional exhaustion, repetitive negativity, and feelings of powerlessness.

2. The Experience

Why the Daily News Experience Breaks Down

People don't avoid news because they lack curiosity about the world. They disengage because contemporary news production relies on formats engineered for friction rather than clarity:

Manufactured Outrage & Anxiety

Prime-time shouting panels and sensational clickbait monetize fear and hostility. The resulting coverage leaves viewers anxious and frustrated rather than informed.

Bloated Pacing & Dense Jargon

Forty minutes of repetitive panel debate is often required to glean thirty seconds of substantive facts. Everyday impact gets buried under ideological point-scoring.

3. The Shift

How People Actually Discover Information

The migration is permanent: audiences under 35 have largely abandoned linear broadcast schedules. Daily information discovery now happens natively within vertical mobile feeds, video platforms, creators, and peer messaging channels.

Audience Shift: 350M+ Indians on Vertical Video Feeds

Short-form mobile video has become India's primary digital media interface. When young citizens encounter news, they encounter it as 15–60 second vertical clips on mobile feeds—not 40-minute television broadcasts.

4. The Quality Challenge

Trust in an Era of Algorithmic Feeds

While mobile feeds solve discovery, they introduce a distinct challenge: fragmented context, unverified claims, and opinion mistaken for factual reporting.

Audiences do not want long-winded lectures, but they demand verifiable credibility. The opportunity is an editorial format that marries the brevity and visual punch of short video with rigorous, primary-source verification.

5. The Opportunity

From Access to Understanding

The fundamental challenge of modern news is no longer publication or distribution. It is building an experience that people genuinely want to engage with, understand, and share.

When news respects the viewer's time, grounds itself in verified data, and communicates with humor, wit, and clarity, news stops being an emotional chore—it becomes an intellectual spark.

6. Our Approach

News, Reimagined

From Humiliation, Shouting to Curiosity, Conversation & Fun.

Khabarwaani is an agentic AI news-reels studio turning complex daily national developments into 15s–60s fact-checked vertical reels across Indian languages. We combine primary-source reporting (gazettes, court filings, official data) with punchy, humorous scripts engineered for mobile completion.
