⚡ Active Development · The Mission & Goal

Why We Are Building Khabarwaani

Khabarwaani is not a completed product—it is actively in development. Our mission is to solve the broken news model. Why can't news be interesting like reels—and funny? Why does news need to be verbose? We are building an agentic newsroom to replace exhausting cable hysteria with crisp, witty, fact-checked vertical reels across Indian languages.

The Four Catalysts Behind What We Are Building

Four urgent structural realities define why Khabarwaani must exist and what we are working to solve.

The Media Crisis & Avoidance Paradox

Millions actively avoid daily news because cable has degraded into manufactured panic and toxic shouting. We are building Khabarwaani to prove that current affairs can inform without exhausting the audience.

The Structural Reset

Why Broadcast TV Fails vs. Why Our Goal Wins

Why the old model cannot be patched—and why rebuilding news as funny, verified reels is our goal.

Why Broadcast TV Is Failing

  • ✕ Manufactured Outrage: Pitting guests against each other for TRP ratings
  • ✕ Verbose Pacing: 40 minutes of talking heads to share 30 seconds of facts
  • ✕ Wrong Viewport: Expecting mobile-native youth to sit in front of cable TV
  • ✕ Crippling Overhead: Millions spent on physical vanity studios and anchors

What We Are Actively Building

  • ✓ Positive Emotion: Smart wit and relatable analogies that lower intellectual defenses
  • ✓ Atomic Brevity: 15s–60s crisp reels with zero throat-clearing fluff
  • ✓ Native Mobile: 9:16 vertical layout delivered directly into social feeds and DMs
  • ✓ Agentic Scale: 90% cost cut, <5 min turnaround, and deterministic fact verification
