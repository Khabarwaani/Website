Audience Reality

The Discovery-to-Trust Deficit

India has 350M+ daily short-form video consumers, with over 72% of Gen Z and younger Millennials consuming news primarily on mobile feeds (Instagram, YouTube Shorts, regional video apps). Yet, the ecosystem is plagued by an acute credibility crisis.

The Discovery Pattern Mass Attention

Over 65% of young audiences consume short news clips on mobile feeds daily. Legacy text newspapers and 40-minute cable broadcasts fail to reach them at their point of discovery.

Attention has permanently migrated to the vertical feed.

The Trust Gap Fewer than 20% Trust

Studies indicate that while over 65% consume short news clips, fewer than 20% trust unverified social commentary, sensationalized talking heads, or clickbait influencers.

Audiences crave brevity, but reject unsubstantiated opinion.
The Khabarwaani Arbitrage:

Khabarwaani captures this vast, unmet demand by pairing vertical video velocity with verifiable sourcing. Viewers get the rapid, engaging 9:16 mobile format they love, backed by an autonomous fact-auditing mesh and primary source trail they can trust.

Engagement Quality

2.5x to 3x higher saves and shares

Not all views are equal. Reaction reels and breaking-opinion shorts trigger ephemeral, low-intent clicks. In contrast, data-backed, educational news explainers generate 2.5x to 3x higher saves and shares:

High Utility = Saves (Bookmarks)

When viewers learn how a new policy affects their taxes, how a tech breakthrough works, or what an ISRO mission means, they bookmark it to refer back to and implement in real life.

Peer Distribution = Organic Shares

Verified, concise explainers become currency in WhatsApp family groups and direct messages. Viewers share them as authoritative explanations that settle questions and inform peers.
