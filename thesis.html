The Core Question

Why

People have 24/7 access to news, yet millions actively avoid it. News doesn't need to be exhausting, shouting, or verbose.

Watch Reel Pilot on Instagram ↗ See the Breakdown
The Paradox

39% Actively Avoid News

Global news avoidance is at an all-time high. Viewers tune out not from lack of curiosity, but from emotional exhaustion, repetitive negativity, and toxic shouting.

Source: Reuters Institute Digital News Report
The Shift

350M+ Indians on Vertical Feeds

Attention has permanently migrated to 15s–60s vertical video. Young audiences discover daily news on mobile feeds, creators, and WhatsApp—not 40-minute cable panels.

Source: IAMAI / Kantar Internet in India
The Contrast

Legacy News vs. Khabarwaani

Legacy Broadcast News
