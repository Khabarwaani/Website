Audience Reality

The Discovery-to-Trust Deficit

Over 350M+ Indians consume information primarily through mobile feeds. Yet the short-video ecosystem is plagued by unverified rumors and opinion clips, while traditional TV news has lost public credibility through toxic screaming.

The Discovery Pattern Mass Attention

Young audiences consume short video clips on mobile feeds daily. Legacy 40-minute cable broadcasts fail to reach them at their point of discovery.

Attention has permanently migrated to the vertical feed.

The Trust Gap Credibility Vacuum

Audiences reject sensationalized talking heads, biased drama, and clickbait influencers. They crave brevity, but demand verifiable truth.

Audiences crave brevity, but reject unsubstantiated hysteria.
The Khabarwaani Arbitrage:

Khabarwaani captures this vast, unmet demand by pairing vertical video velocity with verifiable sourcing. Viewers get the rapid, engaging 9:16 mobile format they love, backed by fact-checked primary sources they can trust.

Retention & Quality

Storytelling engineered for mobile completion

Information is only valuable if it is watched and understood. Mobile users drop off rapidly when presented with television clips retrofitted into vertical crops or bloated talking-head segments.

High Utility = Saves (Bookmarks)

When viewers learn how a new policy affects their taxes, how a tech breakthrough works, or what a court ruling means, they bookmark it to refer back to and use in everyday life.

Peer Distribution = Organic Shares

Verified, concise explainers become currency in WhatsApp family groups and messaging threads. Viewers share them as authoritative summaries that settle questions and inform peers.

Studio Model

Agile, agentic production built for scale

Traditional broadcast newsrooms operate with massive studio overhead, rigid linear schedules, and siloed regional bureaus. Khabarwaani re-engineers news production with specialized AI workflows:

Turnaround Velocity

Direct Signal to Publish

From verified primary documents, court filings, or wire reports to a broadcast-ready 9:16 vertical reel without multi-day shoot delays.

Language Economics

Scalable Vernacular Reach

Adapting verified core reporting across Indian regional languages with natural speech synthesis, avoiding duplicate physical studio costs.

Human + AI Focus

Editorial Governance

Specialized agents automate layout, subtitles, and audio mixing; human editors concentrate fully on journalistic accuracy, ethics, and tone.
