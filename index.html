The Purpose: Why News Can Be Short & Funny Why

Why does news need to be verbose? Millions love scrolling reels because they are fast, visual, and entertaining. News doesn't have to be shouting TV panels or boring jargon. We make verified news engaging, witty, and easy to understand.

The Engine: Agentic AI Meets Editorial Wit How

Automate fact-auditing and vertical production. Cross-reference official gazettes, court filings, and verified wires to filter fake news. Generate 9:16 scenes, witty scripts, and authentic regional voice tracks across Indian languages.

The Product: 10s–60s Multilingual Vertical Reels What

Khabarwaani Reels: One core verified story, zero fluff. Sharp, funny, fact-audited vertical explainers built natively for mobile feeds, published across regional languages in Bharat.

The Feedback Loop: Audience Retention Telemetry The Loop

Real audience metrics calibrate the desk. Viewer completion curves and fact audits continuously refine hook pacing, tone, and humor on our live Instagram pilot (@khabarwaani).

01 · Why Why

Why News Doesn't Need to Be Verbose

Why can't news be as interesting and funny as the reels people watch every day? Staying informed shouldn't mean enduring 40 minutes of screaming anchors or wading through 1,000-word jargon.

Over 90% of new mobile users in India consume content in regional languages. News should be concise, entertaining, and accessible in the format they love.

02 · How How

How Agentic AI Powers the Desk

We anchor every story in verified primary records—official gazettes, legal rulings, and reputable news wires—discarding unverified social rumors.

Our AI workflow assists in drafting witty, hook-first scripts, programmatic 9:16 scene blocking, and natural regional voice synthesis across Indian languages.

What We Deliver: Khabarwaani Reels

10s–60s fact-checked vertical reels built natively for the mobile scroll. 1 core verified fact + clear real-world consequences, delivered with wit and visual clarity.
