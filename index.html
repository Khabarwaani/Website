The Strategic Framework · Simon Sinek's Golden Circle

Why, How, What & The Continuous Feedback Loop

Purpose (Why) dictates Process (How), generating the Product (What), calibrated by real audience feedback (The Loop).
The Purpose · Why Why

• Stop boring, verbose 40-minute shouting panels.
• Shift from negative outrage to positive emotion and wit.
• Bring verified truth to the 350M+ Indians scrolling reels.

The Engine · How How

• Cross-reference official gazettes, court filings, and wires.
• Craft witty, hook-first scripts with zero filler and comedy.
• Programmatic 9:16 vertical motion and regional voice synthesis.

The Product · What What

• 10s–60s fact-checked vertical reels built for mobile feeds.
• 1 verified fact + clear real-world consequences.
• Multilingual releases across Indian regional languages.

The Feedback Loop · Telemetry The Loop

• Viewer completion curves continuously refine pacing and hooks.
• Fact-checking audits prevent misinformation before publishing.
• Active public testing on Instagram (@khabarwaani).
