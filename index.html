01 · Why Why

Why News Must Be Short, Funny & Positive

  • Boring by design: Monotonous anchors reading dry, repetitive teleprompters.
  • Excessive verbosity: Simple policy updates buried under 1,000-word walls of jargon.
  • Gen Z disconnect: Young audiences refuse 40-minute shouting debates for 30 seconds of facts.
  • Loss of trust: Sensationalized clickbait and biased panel drama ruined public credibility.
  • Positive virality: News needs virality, but virality works better with humor, wit, and optimism than toxic anger.
  • Native to the feed: Over 350M+ Indians consume information via vertical reels; news must meet them there.
  • Regional Bharat: 90%+ of new mobile users consume vernacular languages, not elite English.
02 · How How

How Agentic AI Powers Positive News

  • Primary source auditing: Cross-referencing official gazettes, court filings, and wires to kill rumors.
  • Hook-first scripting: Crafting 15s–60s narrative arcs with zero padding and tight pacing.
  • Humor & relatable analogies: Turning complex science and economics into witty, enjoyable explainers.
  • Positive emotional framing: Highlighting solutions, wonder, and tech breakthroughs over doom-scrolling.
  • Programmatic 9:16 vertical staging: Automated vertical scene composition and motion graphics.
  • Authentic regional voices: Natural speech synthesis in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, and English.
  • Rapid turnaround: Compressing days of manual shoot-and-edit cycles into automated hours.
03 · What What

What We Deliver: Khabarwaani Reels

  • 10s–60s vertical reels: Full-screen 9:16 explainers built natively for Instagram and YouTube Shorts.
  • High-signal + fun: 1 core verified fact + clear takeaway + engaging comedy.
  • Zero yelling anchors: Calm, witty, and engaging narration with zero manufactured hysteria.
  • Multilingual releases: One verified story published across Indian regional languages.
  • Instant clarity: Answers "What happened?" and "Why does it matter to me?" in under a minute.
  • Active Instagram pilot: Testing retention, formats, and humor live on @khabarwaani.
  • Owned platform vision: Expanding into an autonomous, trusted vertical newsroom app for Bharat.
