Why, How, What & The Continuous Feedback Loop

Purpose (Why) dictates Process (How), generating the Product (What), continuously calibrated by real-time audience telemetry (The Loop).
The Purpose Why

Stop TV screaming and social fake news. Bring verified truth to the 350M+ Indians who consume news on mobile feeds.

The Engine How

Automate video editing with 7 AI agents. Replace 48-hour manual production friction with 2-hour automated reel generation.

Khabarwaani: 10s–60s fact-checked vertical reels. One verified core story, published simultaneously across 6 Indian languages.

Audience retention telemetry. Viewer completion curves and fact audits continuously recalibrate prompt compilers and pacing.
