The problem

Attention is abundant. Understanding is not.

  • Important stories are often too long, too technical, or too disconnected from everyday Indian life
  • Short-form content is engaging, but frequently loses context, accuracy, and trust
  • Traditional newsrooms are expensive to run at the speed and volume mobile audiences expect
The cost problem

Human production cost multiplies with every language

A single verified story is cheap to report once. The cost explodes when a newsroom tries to re-shoot, re-write, translate, and re-voice that same story for every language and platform it needs to reach. That multiplication — not the reporting itself — is what makes multi-language short-form news expensive and slow to scale. Khabarwaani is built to break that multiplication.

Vernacular Reach

India's hardest distribution problem is our largest opportunity

The verified source story is created once, then adapted into multiple Indian languages without rebuilding the editorial process from zero each time. The target operating model is near-zero incremental human translation cost — people set the truth, tone, and editorial standard once; technology carries the adaptation.

One verified story → many language editions → one shared brand system.
