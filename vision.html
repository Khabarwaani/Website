Current State LEGACY BROADCAST

The Broken News Experience

  • ✕ 40 Minutes for 30s Facts: Shouting panels, repetitive chatter, and manufactured outrage to fill airtime.
  • ✕ 39% News Avoidance: Mental exhaustion and hostility driving young audiences away from news (Reuters Institute Digital News Report 2024).
  • ✕ Dense, Outdated Jargon: Bureaucratic scripts detached from everyday citizens on mobile feeds.
  • ✕ Bloated Studio Overhead: Millions locked into physical studios, anchor salaries, and 4-hour production delays.
Result: Plummeting trust, disconnect from youth (even though youth 15–30 contribute 29% TV news share; BARC India), and irrelevance to mobile users.

The Reimagined Mobile Newsroom

  • ✓ 10s–60s Atomic Vertical Reels: 1 verified fact per reel, kinetic captions, and relatable real-world impact.
  • ✓ Curiosity, Wit & Positive Emotion: Zero panic and screaming anchors, replaced by intelligent humor.
  • ✓ Autonomous Multi-Agent Velocity: Wire-to-reel turnaround in <5 min with 100% primary-source grounding.

Result: High public trust, massive mobile engagement across 600M+ short-video consumers (Bain & Company), and software margins.
