Engine 01 · Scripting App HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP

The AI Agent Scripting App

An autonomous editorial intelligence app that transforms raw, breaking headlines into witty, fact-checked short video scripts (15s–60s) calibrated for mobile retention.

  • 1. News Analysis: Continuously monitors primary wires (PTI, ANI) and official records, stripping away clickbait.
  • 2. Emotion-Driven Scripting: Crafts conversational scripts engineered for curiosity, wit, and relatable analogies.
  • 3. Human Override: Journalists have complete veto power to read, verify, refine, or override any sentence.

Engine 02 · Motion Synthesis BUILT FOR HINDI

The End-to-End Hindi Video Builder

An automated video editing and motion synthesis solution built specifically for Hindi news reels. Compiles approved scripts into broadcast-grade 9:16 vertical videos in under 5 minutes.

  • 1. Automated 9:16 Canvas: Generates beat-matched vertical scenes, editorial b-roll, and visual anchors.
  • 2. Devanagari Kinetic Captions: Synced animated word-by-word subtitles tuned for silent mobile scrolling.
  • 3. Colloquial Hindi Voiceover: Natural neural voice synthesis with conversational timing and 90% lower unit costs.
