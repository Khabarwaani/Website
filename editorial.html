Editorial Verification & Standards

Inspect the journalistic firewall, beat checklists, and primary records standards that ensure every Khabarwaani reel is bulletproof.

Deterministic Integrity

The Journalistic Firewall: Primary Records Before Compute

News is not creative fiction. Every assertion must be verified against official primary records before entering the video and audio synthesis pipeline.

Rule 01 · Signal Parsing

Claim Isolation & Deconstruction

Parses incoming documents into discrete verifiable claims (dates, numbers, direct quotes, statutory citations). Discards sensational commentary and rhetorical fluff.

Rule 02 · Source Corroboration

Primary Document Cross-Check

Cross-references claims against published government gazettes, judicial dockets, and regulator filings—never secondary tabloid summaries or viral social media posts.

Rule 03 · Quality Gate

Deterministic Confidence Gate

Statements are tagged with strict confidence scores. Any assertion with less than 99% primary-source certainty triggers an automatic halt for human editor resolution.

Rule 04 · Ethics

Context & Nuance Protection

Automated guardrails prevent out-of-context video cuts, distorted headlines, or unverified claims designed for cheap virality.

The 60-Second Editorial Test:

"Does this development meaningfully alter what an everyday citizen needs to know, spend, or decide today?" If yes, explain it with strict neutrality, complete accuracy, and zero condescension in under 60 seconds.

Intake Hierarchy

Verification Beat Checklist: What We Monitor

Comprehensive surveillance of primary records across India's five core civic and economic pillars.

National Beat Monitored Primary Sources Editorial Angle
Public Affairs & Law Supreme Court registries, Parliament bills, Gazette of India, Election Commission Translating legal nuance, judicial precedent, and civic rights into plain language.
Money & Economy Reserve Bank of India (RBI), Ministry of Finance, GST Council, SEBI disclosures Converting macroeconomic interest rates and tax revisions into household pocket impact.
Science & Frontier Tech ISRO press archives, patent registries, MeitY policies, peer-reviewed journals Explaining technological capability and national infrastructure without dumbing down the science.
Health & Public Welfare Ministry of Health, ICMR advisories, environmental monitoring agencies (CPCB) Focusing on consumer health, air quality, food safety standards, and citizen well-being.
Culture & Digital Trends Sports regulatory bodies, consumer protection notices, digital platform policies Analyzing viral internet phenomena through sociology and law rather than celebrity gossip.
Intake Grounding Rule:

We do not publish breaking gossip, speculative unconfirmed tweets, or anonymous hearsay. If an alleged story cannot be verified against an official document or authenticated record, the Editorial Desk rejects it.

Transparency & Accountability

Real-Time Fact Auditing, Primary Citations & Corrections

Public accountability is baked directly into the video canvas, caption layer, and pinned docket.

Transparency 01

On-Screen Citation Badges

Every reel displays a persistent primary-source attribution badge (e.g., PIB Gazette #102/2026 or Supreme Court SLP 412) allowing viewers to cross-verify facts instantly.

Transparency 02

Pinned Reference Docket

Captions and pinned comments link directly to the underlying official gazettes, court orders, or economic datasets for viewers seeking full institutional context.

Transparency 03

Instant Erratum & Sub-Clip Patch Protocol

When an official body issues an update or clarification, Khabarwaani doesn't leave outdated news on the feed. The orchestrator isolates the affected 3-second shot, updates the factual overlay, re-renders the cut in under 2 minutes, and pins an explicit clarification notice.

Public Correction Standard:

Traditional media buries errata in tiny footnotes days later. Khabarwaani treats corrections with equal algorithmic prominence, upholding public trust as our ultimate moat.
