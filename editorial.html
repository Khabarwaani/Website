Editorial Standard

Clarity over noise, verified at mobile speed

The modern news feed is flooded with sensationalized outrage and technical jargon that alienates readers. The Editorial Desk filters out noise to focus on stories that change household budgets, rights, technology access, and national capability.

The Editorial Test: Does this development alter what an everyday citizen needs to know, decide, or understand today? If yes, how do we explain it with strict neutrality, complete accuracy, and zero condescension in under 60 seconds?

Trust Architecture

Fact-Auditing & Verification Framework

Before any narrative draft moves into audio and video production, it passes through our Fact-Auditing & Verification Framework:

  • Claim Isolation: Extracts every factual assertion, number, date, and quote into standalone verifiable claims.
  • Source Corroboration: Automatically matches claims against official releases, government datasets, and primary source documents.
  • Confidence Scoring: Tags statements with certainty scores; ambiguities require explicit editor resolution before proceeding.
  • Context Protection: Prevents out-of-context audio/visual juxtapositions that might distort reality for quick clicks.
