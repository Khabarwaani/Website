Guiding Values

What we stand for

1. Truth Before Outrage

We do not amplify manufactured polarization. We ground every story in primary data, legal text, and verified evidence.

2. Relentless Clarity

If a story cannot be explained simply in everyday language without jargon, we haven't understood it deeply enough.

3. Cultural & Vernacular Fluency

Translation is not mere dictionary conversion — it requires honoring local idioms, relatable references, and natural vocal pacing.

4. Human Judgment with AI Scale

Machines handle transcription, rendering, and compositing; humans govern ethics, fact verification, and tone.
