Three Structural Forces Defining Khabarwaani

Why building an autonomous, humor-driven news reels network is a massive commercial and civic opportunity.

Catalyst 01 · Market Size $10B VACUUM

The $10B Mobile Attention Vacuum

Over 700 million Indians consume video daily on smartphones. As youth abandon linear television, hundreds of millions of daily watch hours are migrating to short-form feeds with virtually zero trusted, native news alternatives.

Catalyst 02 · Disruption 10x SOFTWARE UNIT ECONOMICS

10x Unit Economics via Agentic Mesh

Legacy television bureaus require physical studios, massive crews, and millions in annual overhead. Khabarwaani compiles verified, multilingual 9:16 reels in under 5 minutes at a 90% lower unit production cost.

Catalyst 03 · Democratic Moat PUBLIC TRUST

Restoring Public Truth Without Toxicity

Manufactured outrage drives 39% of citizens into active news avoidance. By substituting screaming panels with humor, relatable analogies, and deterministic primary citations, we make staying informed enjoyable again.
