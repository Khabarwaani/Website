Publishing Rhythm

Three daily slots tailored to mobile routines

Mobile discovery is not random — it aligns with predictable commute, lunch, and wind-down cycles. Khabarwaani programs content to fit these exact mindsets.

12-Month Direction

What we are actively building toward

  • Establish an unbroken daily publishing cadence across Hindi and English.
  • Expand into high-demand regional editions (Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi).
  • Scale a searchable website archive of reels and source trails for persistent SEO discovery.
  • Empirically optimize watch time, completion rates, and share ratios.
  • Launch our first partner-led institutional explainer series.
