Vernacular Scale

The Indian Language Scale Matrix

India is not a monolingual broadcast market. Our neural audio and scripting pipeline generates simultaneous vernacular adaptations across key linguistic belts.

Language 01 · Northern & Central Belt

Hindi & Hinglish

Primary national reach, urban conversational banter, relatable colloquial punchlines, and cultural idioms bridging Hindi heartland and cosmopolitan youth.

Language 02 · Western Belt

Gujarati

High-affinity financial and trade explainers, MSME regulatory insights, taxation changes, and entrepreneurial ecosystem breakdowns.

Language 03 · Western Belt

Marathi

State legislative nuance, urban infrastructure development, agricultural policy, and Mumbai-Pune financial corridor updates.

Language 04 · Southern Belt

Tamil & Telugu

Frontier tech innovation, digital governance, state industrial policies, and consumer rights tailored with native southern inflection.

Language 05 · Pan-India Global

English (Indian Context)

Macroeconomic shifts, AI & deep-tech frontier digests, geopolitical analysis, and institutional policy reports for global Indian professionals.

Language 06 · Roadmap Expansion

Bengali, Kannada & Malayalam

Expanding into eastern and southern digital hubs with localized neural voice models trained on regional cadence.

Zero-Dubbing Friction:

Traditional television news spends days and thousands of dollars dubbing packages into regional languages. Khabarwaani compiles regional adaptations in parallel in seconds from the same verified claim docket.

The Anti-Algorithm Rule:

We don't chase platform vanity metrics through outrage bait. We design for completion and shareability: if a reel teaches something valuable with a smile, viewers naturally share it across family and friend networks.

Empirical Refinement

Audience Telemetry & Completion Curves

Every second of watch time is measured to continuously calibrate script pacing, hook efficacy, and visual momentum.

Metric 01

0–3s Hook Velocity

Measuring the thumb-scroll halt rate. We correlate opening question framing with drop-off rates to continually optimize first-second curiosity triggers.

Metric 02

3–18s Explanation Retention

Tracking cognitive comprehension during complex policy and data breakdowns. If viewers exit during a statistical explanation, the scripting agent simplifies the analogy.

Metric 03 · The Sharing Threshold

18–30s Punchline & Share Action

Reaching the end is not enough—viewers must feel a sense of clarity and delight. When completion crosses 85%, the share rate on WhatsApp and social DMs increases 4x.

The Learning Loop:

Audience telemetry is directly connected to our agentic scripting pipeline. Every published reel makes the next reel sharper, faster, and more engaging.
