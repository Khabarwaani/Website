The 3 Studio Synthesis Engines

How Khabarwaani compiles kinetic visual design, regional speech synthesis, and retention pacing into finished mobile reels.

Mobile-Native Visual Engine

Programmatic 9:16 Kinetic Typography & SVG Motion

Over 70% of social media feeds are browsed with sound muted. Our studio engine renders dynamic typography and numeric infographics directly into the vertical video canvas.

Motion 01 · Text Animation

Word-by-Word Kinetic Subtitles

Synchronized with speech phonemes down to the millisecond. Highlights key figures, legal terminology, and witty emphasis in high-contrast color palettes for rapid silent comprehension.

Motion 02 · Data Visualization

Zero-Drift Programmatic SVG Charts

Financial curves, inflation bars, and legislative timelines are compiled programmatically via vector SVGs—eliminating hallucinated visual artifacts or blurry graphs common in standard generative models.

Motion 03 · Mobile Ergonomics

Safe-Zone Boundaries & Camera Focal Locking

All visual elements and typography strictly respect smartphone safe-zones (avoiding Instagram and YouTube UI buttons, like counters, comments, and profile bars) while preserving natural character eye-lines and focal locks.

Feed-Native Vertical Standard:

We don't letterbox a widescreen 16:9 television broadcast into a tiny box on your phone. Every Khabarwaani reel is engineered natively for full-bleed vertical attention.

Vernacular Neural Audio

Regional Voice Synthesis & Colloquial Dialect Tuning

News fails when it sounds like a corporate robot. Khabarwaani tunes neural speech models on natural conversational cadence, humor, and colloquial pauses across Indian languages.

Audio 01 · Vernacular Tuning

Colloquial Dialect Intonation

Synthesizes Hindi, Hinglish, Marathi, Tamil, Bengali, and regional Indian tongues with authentic emotional inflection, witty comedic cadence, and cultural idioms—avoiding robotic monotone.

Audio 02 · Dramatic Timing

Context-Aware Pause Modulation

Inserts micro-pauses (200ms–400ms) before punchlines and statistical reveals to replicate natural human breathing, conversational banter, and dramatic punch.

Production Model Turnaround Speed Unit Cost per Language Cultural Adaptability
Traditional Bureau Dubbing 24–48 hours ₹40,000–₹80,000 / reel Delayed, literal, formal translation
Khabarwaani Neural Mesh < 30 seconds < ₹10 / reel Simultaneous colloquial adaptation across 5+ languages
Multi-Track Vernacular Mesh:

A single verified editorial brief compiles into localized audio tracks simultaneously. A Hindi reel, a Hinglish explainer, and a Tamil breakdown can publish in the exact same minute.

Algorithmic Engagement

Pacing, Cut Frequency & Retention Synchronization

Engineering news to be completed, understood, and shared before the user swipes away on mobile feeds.

Pacing 01

The 3-Second Visual Shift Rule

The human eye fatigues quickly on smartphone feeds. Our compositor orchestrates a camera angle cut, B-roll overlay, or typographic pop every 2.5–3.5 seconds to maintain visual momentum.

Pacing 02

Humor & Analogy Timing Anchors

Punchlines and relatable metaphors are placed at seconds 2, 12, and 24 to reward the viewer's attention and keep cognitive load delightfully light throughout complex stories.

Pacing 03

Closed-Loop Drop-Off Optimization

Real-time telemetry correlates exact drop-off moments with scene cuts. If viewers drop at a specific policy transition, the pacing parameterizer automatically tightens that transition for future reels.

The Completion Rate Moat:

Cable news relies on panic and sensational cliffhangers to delay commercial breaks. Khabarwaani relies on atomic speed and wit so citizens willingly finish 100% of the explainer.
