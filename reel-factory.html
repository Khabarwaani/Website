Case Study

The SPADEX Story: Space physics made human

When ISRO prepares a complex orbital docking experiment, traditional coverage either defaults to impenetrable technical jargon or 5-second sensationalized clips. Khabarwaani's Reel Factory packages it as high-stakes national drama:

Core Narrative: "Docking in the Dark"

"India is learning to bring two spacecraft together in orbit at 28,000 km/h — and that single capability unlocks space stations, moon return missions, and satellite servicing."

Scene 1: Mission-control tension and orbital scale — visualizing two dots converging in space.
Scene 2: The practical payoff — what this means for India's upcoming Bharatiya Antariksh Station.
Outcome: Scripted once, rendered across Hindi, English, and regional editions with native voice synthesis in under 4 hours.
