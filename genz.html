Where Young Audiences Actually Live

The disconnect between newsrooms and youth isn't an interest deficit—it's a medium and cultural mismatch.

Shift 01

Print & TV News is Dead for Under-30s

Nobody under 30 buys morning newspapers or sits through 9 PM TV shouting broadcasts. The median cable news viewer is 55+. Physical print circulation among Gen Z is near zero—attention lives 100% in mobile vertical feeds.

Shift 02

The 'Preachy Uncle' Host Disconnect

Legacy news reels and TV broadcasts are produced by 40–50 year olds lecturing with outdated jargon. Young audiences instantly swipe away condescending uncle gyaan. They want smart, witty peers who talk like internet natives.

Shift 03

Vertical Feeds & 2-Second Retention

Over 70% of news discovery among 18–24 year-olds happens in 9:16 mobile feeds. Viewers decide to stay in under 2 seconds. Anchor self-introductions, title graphics, and filler copy get swiped away instantly.
