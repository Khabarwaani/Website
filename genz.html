Structural Attention Shift

Where Young Audiences Actually Live

The disconnect between newsrooms and youth is a medium and cultural mismatch, not a lack of interest.

Shift 01

Print & TV News is Dead for Under-30s

Nobody under 30 buys newspapers or watches 9 PM shouting shows. Youth (15–30) are the top TV news contributors at 29% share (BARC India), yet attention lives entirely on vertical mobile feeds.

Shift 02

The 'Preachy Uncle' Host Disconnect

Broadcasts feature older anchors lecturing with outdated jargon. Young audiences quickly swipe away condescending commentary. They want sharp peers who speak like internet natives.

Shift 03

Vertical Feeds & 2-Second Retention

71% of Indians prefer online news; 54% via YouTube; 79% on mobile (Reuters Institute 2024). Viewers decide to stay within 2 seconds. Long intros and filler copy get swiped away immediately.
