Six Stages

Six moments in the Khabarwaani experience

Speed without casualness. Every story travels through a deliberate sequence designed to maximize retention while preserving strict journalistic grounding.

01

Find the signal

Stories with consequence: identifying something that meaningfully changes what people know, spend, discuss, or expect in their daily lives.

02

Earn trust

Cross-checked, verified, and compared against primary documents, official data, and beat reporting before being shaped for mobile attention.

03

Find the human meaning

A complicated policy or scientific milestone becomes a relatable, everyday question: \"What does this actually change for me?\"

04

Make it understandable

Scripted specifically for natural spoken cadence and short viewing windows: 10, 15, 30, or 60 seconds — structured with zero fluff.

05

Make it memorable

A sharp first-second hook, visual turns, clean typography overlays, sound design cues, and a compelling reason to complete and share.

06

Protect the promise

Multi-layer review for factual accuracy, nuance, tone, continuity, accessibility, and audio before publishing across regional editions.

The Story Package

A complete, ready-to-watch story unit

A Khabarwaani reel is not raw footage cut down to vertical — it is an engineered information product built for the phone screen.

  • First-second hook: Instantly establishes relevance before thumb-scroll drop-off
  • Regional language clarity: Native Hindustani and regional adaptations with cultural context
  • Phone-first cinematic visuals: 9:16 vertical compositions with legible typographic anchors
  • Grounded source trail: Verified context and references attached to every explainer
  • Contextual thumbnail & caption: Extends the story for viewers seeking deeper nuance
  • High-retention loop: Built to be finished, remembered, and shared across WhatsApp and social feeds
