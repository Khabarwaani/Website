Operational Lifecycle

The 6 Production Stages

Khabarwaani breaks news compilation into three distinct two-stage operational cycles.

Intake & Creative Reasoning

Stages 1 & 2: Automated Ingestion & Comedy Scriptwriting Hooks

Transforming raw legislative gazettes, court orders, and economic wires into atomic, high-retention vertical scripts.

Stage 01 · Signal Intake REAL-TIME SIGNAL

Signal Ingestion & Atomic Extraction

Monitors official gazettes, court registries, and financial regulators. Strips speculation and extracts verifiable atomic claims with origin timestamps and legal authority tiers.

  • ✓ Continuous parsing of PIB, Supreme Court, and RBI wires
  • ✓ De-duplication against active story clusters
  • ✓ Generation of structured claim vectors
Stage 02 · Script Synthesis 10s–60s VERTICAL ARC

Scriptwriting with Comedy Hooks

Rewrites complex policy into conversational vertical scripts. Replaces dry headlines with immediate, relatable questions: "What does this actually cost your wallet tomorrow?"

  • ✓ First-second hook formula prevents thumb-scroll drop-off
  • ✓ Relatable cultural analogies and witty punchlines
  • ✓ Strict word-budget enforcement for rapid pacing
The 3-Part Atomic Reel Script Structure (30s format):
0.0s – 3.0s · The Hook (30s format)

Unexpected question or witty observation that arrests feed scrolling.

3.0s – 18.0s · The Core Fact (30s format)

Unpacking the primary source truth using simple metaphors and clear graphics.

18.0s – 30.0s · The Punchline (30s format)

Memorable takeaway and actionable reality check that sparks comments & shares.

Audit & Visual Motion

Stages 3 & 4: Fact-Auditing & 9:16 Kinetic Rendering

Deterministic fact verification before any compute is spent, followed by programmatic 9:16 mobile canvas compilation.

Stage 03 · Verification Gate 100% GROUNDED

Fact-Auditing & Legal Firewall

Every line of script is cross-referenced against primary source files. The automated firewall scans for defamation, legal compliance, and citation accuracy before generation.

  • ✓ Verification against official gazette PDFs and case dockets
  • ✓ Rejection of unconfirmed gossip and speculative clickbait
  • ✓ Mandatory human escalation if source confidence < 99%
Stage 04 · Visual Generation PROGRAMMATIC 9:16

9:16 Kinetic Rendering

Shot lists are compiled into structured JSON scene parameters. High-speed SVG charts and animated kinetic typography are rendered natively into the vertical mobile canvas.

  • ✓ Deterministic focal lengths and facial consistency anchors
  • ✓ Programmatic SVG numeric charts with zero AI visual drift
  • ✓ High-contrast animated kinetic subtitles for silent viewing
Why Deterministic Auditing Precedes Rendering:

By enforcing factual audits at Stage 3 before generating audio or rendering pixels, Khabarwaani prevents wasted GPU compute on unverified stories and guarantees that hallucinations never reach the public.

Scale & Telemetry Loops

Stages 5 & 6: Multilingual Voice Synthesis & Audience Telemetry

Simultaneous vernacular adaptation across Indian languages paired with closed-loop audience retention measurement.

Stage 05 · Vernacular Audio MULTILINGUAL AUDIO

Multilingual Voice Synthesis

Synthesizes conversational Indian neural voices across Hindi, Hinglish, Marathi, Tamil, and Bengali. Modulates breathing, dramatic pauses, and colloquial punchlines.

  • ✓ Native dialect intonations without robotic monotone
  • ✓ Automatic audio-visual lip-sync synchronization
  • ✓ Simultaneous release across Indian vernacular channels
Stage 06 · Closed-Loop Telemetry RETENTION OPTIMIZATION

Audience Telemetry & QA

Measures second-by-second drop-off across Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Correlates hook retention with script formulas to continually refine newsroom velocity.

  • ✓ Second-by-second drop-off curve diagnostics
  • ✓ Closed-loop tuning of humor analogies and visual pacing
  • ✓ Instant sub-clip patching if corrections are mandated
The Closed-Loop Feedback Engine:

Audience telemetry doesn't just measure views—it measures comprehension and completion. When data reveals that viewers drop off at a specific statistical explanation, the scripting agent automatically simplifies the analogy for the next story package.
