Execution Reality · Active Today

Live Hypothesis & Reach Testing on Instagram

Rather than building software in a silo or relying on hypothetical focus groups, Khabarwaani is actively testing its format, pacing, and editorial voice directly in the wild on Instagram (@khabarwaani).

Core Hypothesis Under Test

Does Positive Emotion Beat TV Panic?

Legacy media assumes Indian audiences only respond to fear, religious division, and sensational outrage. Our live pilot tests whether young Indian mobile viewers engage deeper with witty, verified, and constructive news delivered in 15s–60s vertical reels.

  • ✓ Testing thumb-stop hook rates in the first 3 seconds
  • ✓ Measuring video completion rates against industry benchmarks
  • ✓ Tracking voluntary saves and peer-to-peer DM sharing
Operational Feedback Loop

Audience Data Guides the AI Engines

Every real reel published provides live telemetry that immediately improves our two engines:

  • • Scripting Agent Tuning: Calibrating joke subtlety, slang naturalness, and reading speed based on viewer drop-offs.
  • • Video Builder Refinement: Improving subtitle font weights, Devanagari kerning, and visual transition tempos.
  • • Community Sentiment: Analyzing comment sections to uncover civic topics young citizens actually care about.

The Funding Horizon · Scaling the Software

What Securing Funding Unlocks

Today, our two core engines are operational prototypes driven by founder execution. Securing seed funding will transform this validated workflow into a full-scale autonomous software application and distribution platform.

Milestone 01 · Software Platform CORE DELIVERABLE

Autonomous SaaS Application & Newsroom Dashboard

Building the self-serve web application for journalists, independent creators, and media organizations. Includes real-time wire ingestion monitors, the collaborative emotion-scripting editor, side-by-side fact citation checkers, and one-click video builder dispatch.

Milestone 02 · Cloud Infrastructure PARALLEL RENDERING

Scalable Cloud GPU Video Synthesis Pipeline

Transitioning video rendering to dedicated distributed cloud GPU instances. This enables simultaneous rendering of 50+ vertical news reels across breaking news cycles, dropping average wire-to-publish turnaround to under 3 minutes.

Milestone 03 · Regional Expansion MULTILINGUAL EXPANSION

Expanding the Video Builder to Additional Indian Languages

While Engine 2 is currently built specifically for Hindi, funding will finance linguistic modeling, native typography engines, and regional voiceover synthesis for Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil, and Telugu.
