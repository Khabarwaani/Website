The 39% News Avoidance Paradox

Global news access is at an all-time high, yet 39% of audiences actively avoid the news. The public hasn't lost curiosity—they are exhausted by manufactured outrage and toxic yelling.

Root Cause Analysis

Why Viewers Are Tuning Out

Audiences don't dislike current affairs. They reject the anxiety-inducing, click-hungry format of broadcast media.

Driver 01

Manufactured Outrage for TRP

Legacy cable networks optimize for anger because fear drives continuous session duration. By pitting shouting panellists against each other, they manufacture conflict where none exists.

Reuters Institute Digital News Report 2024
Driver 02

Exhausting Verbosity

Traditional broadcasts fill 24-hour programming slots with repetitive teleprompter scripts, long throat-clearing introductions, and endless jargon that wastes viewers' time.

Khabarwaani Research & Attention Audit
Driver 03

The Negativity Trap

Over 70% of news avoiders cite negative mood effects. Relentless crisis framing without explanation, humor, or real-world consequence leaves citizens depressed and disengaged.

Oxford / Reuters Global Survey
The Khabarwaani Reset

Truth Delivered with Wit

Replace screaming with smart humor, replace 40-minute rants with 30-second verified reels, and replace manufactured panic with curiosity, clarity, and conversation.

The Format Shift

Cable Outrage vs. Verified Reels

Why changing the emotion changes everything.

Legacy Broadcast Model

  • ✕ 40 minutes of panel shouting to communicate 30s of facts
  • ✕ Manufactured outrage, fear, and hostility to protect TRP
  • ✕ Dense jargon, repetitive teleprompter scripts, and fluff
  • ✕ Results in viewer fatigue, cynicism, and active avoidance

Khabarwaani Reels Model

  • ✓ 15s–60s fact-checked vertical reels built natively for mobile
  • ✓ Curiosity, wit, humor, and positive emotion instead of yelling
  • ✓ 1 verified claim + clear consequences + zero filler sentences
  • ✓ High viral shareability across family and friend networks
