Operational Workflow

How the AI Scripting Agent Works

From raw wire alert to human-approved video script in three clear stages.

Stage 01 · Signal Ingestion FACT ISOLATION

1. News Analysis & Primary Record Ingestion

The agent does not browse opinion blogs or social media gossip. It connects directly to primary source feeds: news agency wires (PTI, ANI), Press Information Bureau (PIB) gazettes, Ministry circulars, and Supreme Court rulings.

Noise & Clickbait Filtering

Strips away manufactured outrage, hyperventilating adjectives, partisan spin, and rhetorical questions intended to induce anxiety.

Fact Extraction Matrix

Extracts the verified core event, dates, exact statutory figures, and direct impact on the everyday citizen's pocketbook or civil rights.

Stage 02 · Emotion Scripting POSITIVE POLARITY

2. Emotion-Driven Scriptwriting for Short Video (10s–60s)

Traditional news television uses fear and aggression to trap viewers in commercial loops. Khabarwaani reverses this dynamic by engineering scripts for positive emotion: curiosity, wit, and conversational clarity. The script is structured strictly for vertical phone scrolling (60s format):

0–3s · Thumb-Stop Hook (60s format)

An unexpected question, visual paradox, or witty observation that stops the user's thumb before they swipe past.

4–20s · Core Breakdown (60s format)

Delivers the core facts in punchy, everyday conversational language without academic jargon or bureaucratic throat-clearing.

21–45s · Nuance & Metaphor (60s format)

Grounds the story using relatable cultural analogies and civic context, making complex policy immediately intuitive.

46–60s · Closer & Share Hook (60s format)

A memorable satirical punchline and call-to-conversation that drives high organic save rates and DM shares.

Stage 03 · Editorial Control HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP

3. Human Review, Refinement & Complete Override

Artificial intelligence generates the first draft; human journalists hold absolute editorial veto. The app provides a dedicated editorial review interface before any script is passed to the video builder:

Line-by-Line Fact Audit

Every claim is linked side-by-side with its official document citation. Editors verify figures and context with a single click.

Tone & Punchline Refinement

Editors can adjust jokes, dial up/down satire, tune colloquial Hindi idioms, or completely overwrite lines to suit their editorial standards.
