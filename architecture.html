Core Problem

AI Video Generation Has an Orchestration Problem

News production is a chain of specialized tasks: research, fact verification, scripting, speech synthesis, and scene rendering. Left unconstrained, standalone video models suffer from:

  • Identity drift and wardrobe swaps between adjacent shots
  • Inconsistent character eye-lines, gaze direction, and posture
  • Dialogue pauses that collapse natural spoken rhythm
  • Hallucinated backgrounds and temporal motion artifacts
  • Struggles with conversational turn-taking and reaction shots
The Orchestration Imperative:

The challenge is not waiting for a single monolithic model to do everything. The challenge is building an intelligent orchestration layer that coordinates specialized models with deterministic accuracy.

Specification Layer

Machine-Readable Production Specs

In automated news production, natural language prompts alone are too ambiguous. Khabarwaani compiles creative intent into structured, machine-readable JSON scene parameters:

Ambiguous Text Prompt

Leaves spatial blocking, timing, and continuity to chance.

"Two people discuss the news in an office."

Result: Identity swaps, random angles, overlapping speech, broken continuity.

Khabarwaani Production Spec

Structured parameterization encoding characters, blocking, and timing constraints.

{
  "scene_id": "shot_03_discussion",
  "characters": [
    {"id": "char_A", "role": "lead_anchor", "pose": "seated_desk", "face_seed": "ak_4021"},
    {"id": "char_B", "role": "analyst", "pose": "standing_profile"}
  ],
  "camera": {"angle": "medium_two_shot", "lens_mm": 50, "focal_lock": "char_A"},
  "dialogue_timeline": [
    {"speaker": "char_A", "start_s": 0.0, "end_s": 3.8, "gaze": "char_B"},
    {"pause_ms": 350, "pacing": "natural_breath"},
    {"speaker": "char_B", "start_s": 4.15, "end_s": 8.5, "gaze": "char_A"}
  ],
  "constraints": ["no_direct_camera_gaze", "strict_continuity_bg"]
}

Result: Deterministic blocking, exact speech pauses, and seamless cut transitions.

Orchestration Mesh

Specialized Agent Roles in the Newsroom

Rather than relying on a single generalist model, Khabarwaani distributes production across specialized modules governed by a unified bus:

Module 01

Research & Ingestion

Monitors official wires, court filings, and regulatory gazettes for verified signal.

Module 02

Fact Verification

Audits claims against primary source records and tags statements with confidence checks.

Module 03

Script & Vernacular

Shapes verified claims into 15s–60s spoken vertical arcs with humor, analogies, and zero filler.

Module 04

Scene Staging

Converts script lines into machine-readable shot lists, focal lengths, and camera moves.

Module 05

Model Routing

Dispatches individual scene specs to the optimal video, voice, or motion generators.

Module 06

Quality Assurance

Evaluates rendered clips against spatial, timing, lip-sync, and continuity parameters.

Module 07

Compositing & Export

Assembles validated clips, burns animated kinetic typography, syncs regional audio, and exports final 9:16 cuts.

Operational Efficiency

Dynamic Model Routing & Granular Quality Control

The best model is not the same for every shot. A production studio cannot select the most expensive model for every second of video. Khabarwaani routes tasks dynamically based on complexity and latency:

Scene Type Routing Strategy Engineering Rationale
Simple Talking Head High-speed, low-latency model adapter Prioritizes fast lip-sync alignment and rapid turnaround.
Complex 2-Person Interaction High-fidelity generative model Preserves multi-character spatial continuity, eye-lines, and natural turn-taking.
Data / Infographic Explainer Deterministic motion engine Eliminates hallucination risk; programmatic SVG and numeric visual rendering.
Vernacular Speech Synthesis Dialect-specialized neural TTS Natural spoken cadence with colloquial pause modulation in regional Indian languages.
Targeted Sub-Clip Repair:

When an individual 3-second shot fails a continuity or lip-sync check during automated evaluation, the orchestrator re-renders only that specific sub-clip with tighter constraints — rather than regenerating the entire video.

Journalistic Grounding

Factual Traceability in News Video

News is strictly more demanding than entertainment. While creative video allows artistic liberties, a news explainer must defend every sentence against factual errors and hallucinations.

Fast Corrections & Updates:

Because each script line and video scene is mapped back to its primary source node, breaking updates or official clarifications can be edited and re-rendered in minutes without rebuilding the story from scratch.
