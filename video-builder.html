Why Built Specifically for Hindi

Television Hindi news has devolved into sensory violence: 10-guest screaming panels, flashing emergency sirens, and sensationalist anxiety. Over 500 million Hindi speakers deserve news that is verified, respectful, and genuinely engaging.

  • ✕ Broken Devanagari Typography: Western tools corrupt complex conjuncts (matras and halants), producing garbled text.
  • ✕ Robotic Voice Accents: Monotone text-to-speech models mispronounce common Hindustani words and fail comedic timing.
  • ✕ Alien Visuals: Stock footage algorithms serve irrelevant Western b-roll disconnected from Indian reality.
